# Intended use

opentwin-synthea generates synthetic patient records for research, software
testing and non-medical wellness work. Every person it produces is made up by
the [Synthea](https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea) simulator and says
nothing about any real person.

**Medical use is outside the intended scope:** diagnosis, disease prediction or
prevention, clinical monitoring, triage, treatment or other medical decisions.
The generated records are not clinically validated and must not guide care or
replace professional medical advice.

Operators must display this notice alongside relevant outputs:

> Experimental software for research and non-medical wellness. Not intended for
> diagnosis, treatment or other medical decisions. Outputs may be incomplete or
> incorrect; research scores are not clinically validated assessments.

This repository claims no medical-device certification or clearance. See also
open-twin's [intended use](https://github.com/Opening-Science/open-twin/blob/main/docs/INTENDED-USE.md).
